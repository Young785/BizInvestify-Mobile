Startup Plan: BizInvestify
📅 Vision
A platform that enables individuals to:
	•	Post and sell their products.
	•	List their businesses or business ideas for sale.
	•	Receive investments or partial funding from other users.
	•	Enable full acquisition of their business by interested buyers.
Targeted at small business owners, side hustlers, creators, and investors.

BizInvestify - Startup Platform Plan
Brand Identity
Brand Name: BizInvestify
Tagline: Empowering Product Sellers to Connect with Buyers and Investors Seamlessly.
Color Palette
Primary Color: #1A73E8 (Used for CTAs, links, headers)
Accent Color: #00C48C (Used for success alerts, highlights)
Dark Text: #1C1C1E (For main readable text)
Background: #F5F7FA (Soft light backgrounds and sections)
Platform Overview
BizInvestify is a dual-purpose marketplace that allows individuals to:- List products or businesses they wish to sell.- Attract buyers or investors interested in acquiring or funding those listings.
User Roles and Features
1. Seller Portal
Key Features:- Sign up and create a seller profile.- Upload products or businesses for sale with images, video, descriptions.- Set pricing, equity offered (for investment), or outright sale price.- Dashboard: Track views, offers, chat inquiries, analytics.- Accept, reject or counter investor/buyer proposals.- KYC Verification for trust and fraud prevention.
2. Buyer/Investor Portal
Key Features:- Browse available listings by category, price, location, sector.- Use smart filters (e.g., trending, hot deals, high ROI).- Express interest, offer funding, or buy immediately.- Chat with sellers, view history, check business KPIs.- Save listings, receive notifications, track investments.
3. Admin Dashboard
Key Features:- User management: Approve, suspend or verify accounts.- Listing moderation: Approve or reject new posts.- Payment oversight: Track commissions, subscriptions, payouts.- Analytics Dashboard: User engagement, top sellers, traffic.- Support and ticket management.
Monetization Strategies
- Commission on successful business/product sales (e.g., 2–5%).- Premium Listings: Charge for featured visibility.- Subscription plans for sellers and investors.- Affiliate and advertisement revenue.- Sponsored deals and spotlight investments.
Next Steps
1. Build out landing page in Figma using defined color palette.2. Develop MVP with portals for each role.3. Integrate payment, chat, listing engine, and authentication.4. Start with a niche category (e.g., tech gadgets, mini-businesses).5. Launch closed beta and gather feedback.6. Plan for mobile app and investor discovery AI tools.
